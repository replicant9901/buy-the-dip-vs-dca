# buy-the-dip-vs-dca
Backtesting buy-the-dip vs. dollar-cost averaging on SOXX, QQQ, and S&P 500 (KRW-adjusted)

*
SOXX(iShares Semiconductor ETF): Tracks the NYSE Semiconductor Index, made up of the 30 largest U.S.-listed semiconductor companies. A concentrated, high-volatility sector bet.
QQQ(Invesco QQQ Trust): Tracks the Nasdaq-100, the 100 largest non-financial companies listed on the Nasdaq. Heavily weighted toward big tech.
SPY(SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust): An index of 500 large U.S. companies across all sectors, widely used as the benchmark for the overall U.S. market. Represented here by SPY (SPDR S&P 500 ETF).
